# BeautyConnect AI – Campaign Performance Evaluation

**Task 5 | AI-Powered Beauty & Wellness Campaign Analysis**

## Project Overview

BeautyConnect AI is a hypothetical AI-powered beauty and wellness marketing campaign designed to evaluate the effectiveness of personalized marketing strategies.

This project analyzes simulated campaign data from August to October 2026 to measure campaign performance, evaluate customer engagement, compare AI-personalized marketing with generic marketing, and identify opportunities for improvement.

The project combines data analytics, AI-assisted evaluation, KPI measurement, and business intelligence visualization.

## Campaign Information

| Detail | Description |
|---|---|
| Campaign Name | BeautyConnect AI |

| Project Type | Simulated Educational Project |

| Campaign Comparison | AI Personalized vs Generic Control |
| Product Categories | Skincare, Haircare, Body Care, Wellness |

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Impressions
- Total Clicks
- Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- Conversion Rate
- Total Orders
- Campaign Revenue
- Campaign Expenditure
- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
- Customer Satisfaction
- Repeat Purchase Rate

## Key Findings

- Customer sentiment was predominantly positive.
- Instagram emerged as a leading marketing channel in the simulated analysis.
- Value Seeker and Skincare Obsessed were important customer segments.
- Value bundles and personalized skincare were popular content themes.
- Monthly revenue trends highlighted opportunities for campaign optimization.
- AI personalization was evaluated against generic marketing to assess potential improvements in customer engagement and conversion.

## AI Techniques Used

1. Customer Segmentation
2. Personalized Product Recommendations
3. Predictive Customer Behavior Analysis
4. Sentiment Analysis
5. Marketing Performance Evaluation
6. Comparative Campaign Analysis

## Expected Outcomes

- Evaluate the effectiveness of AI-powered personalization.
- Identify high-performing marketing channels.
- Understand customer preferences and sentiment.
- Measure campaign profitability and conversion performance.
- Recommend strategies for improving future campaigns.

## Disclaimer

This project uses hypothetical and simulated educational data. Campaign results do not represent actual company performance or real customer transactions.
